DEADWEIGHT BEGINNER WEBSITE
===========================

1. Open index.html in a web browser.
2. Open index.html, style.css, or script.js in VS Code to make edits.
3. Keep the images folder next to the three code files.

EDITING CHEAT SHEET
- Text, products, and picture choices: index.html
- Colors, size, spacing, fonts, mobile styles: style.css
- Menu, filters, and email form check: script.js

FONTS
- Barlow Condensed and DM Sans load from Google Fonts.

EMAIL WAITLIST
- Create a form on formspree.io.
- In index.html replace YOUR_FORM_ID with your actual Formspree ID.
- Until then, the form explains that signup is not connected.

LOGO
- images/deadweight-logo.png is a transparent cream-colored version
  of your NEW arched black-on-white upload. No previous logo is used.
- All other photos come from your supplied campaign set.

PRODUCTS
- Product cards say COMING SOON. They don't sell anything yet.
- When ready, connect a real ecommerce checkout before accepting orders.
